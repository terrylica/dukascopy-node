What's in the data column dictionary

tsBar open time, UTC (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS).
bid_open/high/low/closeBid-side OHLC price for the 5-minute bar (split-adjusted).
ask_open/high/low/closeAsk-side OHLC price for the 5-minute bar (split-adjusted).
mid_open/high/low/close(bid + ask) / 2, computed per OHLC field.
bid_volume / ask_volumeDukascopy indicative CFD volume per side (relative activity only).

Data & timestamps read this before using the bars

Each row is one 5-minute bar; the ts column is the bar's OPEN time, in UTC (24-hour clock, no daylight-saving). Because the stamps are UTC, the US regular session lands at 13:30–20:00 UTC in summer (09:30–16:00 New York EDT) and 14:30–21:00 UTC in winter (09:30–16:00 EST) — the 1-hour seasonal shift is real and visible in the data, and confirms the UTC labelling. Nights, weekends and market holidays are gaps, not missing data. Most US names are regular-session; a few (e.g. PLTR, SNOW) carry some extended-hours bars, and the bonus WTAI ETF trades on a European schedule (~08:00–16:25 UTC). Prices are split-adjusted; volume is indicative.
